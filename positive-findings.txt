- **What doesn't work:** DeepSeek with Claude Code solves any fix with one obvious location, usually in 4–27 minutes.
- Both patterns are in the generator's technique guide and failure map.
- **The leak check works:** it caught a released, already-fixed copy of httpx2 inside the image before any model saw it.
