This checkout is compiled offline with the JDK 8 compiler at the project's own
language level:

    /usr/local/bin/tomcat-build /workspace /workspace/output/classes

The helper compiles the java/ tree and copies its resource files. JUnit 4 and
Hamcrest are installed at /usr/share/java/. `ant` is present, but the bundled
commons-dbcp module in this snapshot does not compile against JDK 8.
